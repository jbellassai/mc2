# Scripted quest scenes (program P08)

Sources: `P08.MCO` (`decomp/overlays/P08.c`, asm via `tools/dis.py`), room files `D16` (Castle Oshcrun rooms), `D60` (elden and dungeon scenes), `D36` (Ziyx's, camps, the Oolau bird's room), `D98` (Dark Candle rooms, see `locations.md`). Event numbers as in `docs/events.md`. `%rNN` in a text prints "[Read Paragraph NN.]", a pointer into the printed paragraph booklet (`dialogue.md`, *Text format codes*).

## How a P08 room works
A P08 room is an ordinary room: a `LOCTYPE` from a bank (`D16`/`D36`/`D60`, entry = the door's room id, `ovl_13e9`), its 11×9 map (G-file chunk, `ovl_1507`), its people (`_InitNPC`), Talk, Look, Use, Whisper and exits. The program adds scenes keyed by **(`_locspec`, `_currm`)**, which P08 takes from `_rmdata[7]` and `_rmdata[0]`: `_rmdata[7]` is the `_locspec` of the location the door is in (P02 0x792 and P01 both copy DS:4D3A at the door), `_rmdata[0]` the door's room id. The world map is location 1.

The 18 doors into P08 rooms (`tools/doors.py`): Oshcrun (3) → D16 room 1 (throne room); Cellars 2 (24) → D60 0; Deraum (28) → D60 1; Shann 6 (50) → D60 2 (Maalaq's); Drakhelm 3 (71) → D60 3; Maratul 8 (44) → D60 5; Pentyne (56) → D60 6 (Orb altar); Mandarg 4 (79) → D60 7; Wanasol (56) → D60 8 (meeting hall); and on the world map R36 (47,38) → D36 room 0 (Ziyx's; the square is also a program-8 town entrance). The Oolau bird's room (D36 room 5) has no door or feature in any location file found so far.

**Party placement** (`ovl_1484`): every exit `{x, y, pgm, winx, winy, var}` of the room contributes a side flag (x 0 → 2 W, y 0 → 4 N, x 10 → 8 E, y 8 → 16 S). With more than one exit, `_lastdir` (the last step key − '1': 0 SW, 1 S, 2 SE, 3 W, 5 E, 6 NW, 7 N, 8 NE, set by P02 0x46B) narrows it: orthogonal steps replace the flags with P08 file 0xA3[dir] (`[12,4,6,8,0,2,24,16,18]`, so a step north enters on the south side), diagonal steps AND them with 0xAC[dir/2] (`[12,6,0,24,18]`). The flags pick the formation origin from the word table at file 0x83 (word = flags/2, x low byte): 0 → (4,3), W (1,3), N (4,1), W+N (1,1), E (7,3), N+E (7,1), S (4,5), W+S (1,5), E+S (7,5), anything else (4,3). Members stand on the usual 3×3 formation from it (`_formatn`, P08 0x71/0x7A).

**Leaving**: stepping on an exit asks "Do you want to leave?" (Yes/No) — unless a scroll lies on the floor and no ghost is left, then the cleverest member (assumed `_HighStat(intellect)`) "%o refuses to leave the %a here." (text 11) and nobody leaves. The exit's `pgm` (an index into the room's own PGM list) and `var` (the location's D-file) say where to.

## Port notes
Built in the web port (`web/src/scene.ts`, `main.ts`): placement, the leave prompt and refusal, Ziyx's books, the King's gifts, Maalaq, the priest, the elden brothers, the three ghosts with Soulspeak and Restsoul, the scrolls on the floor, the White Scroll's council transport, the Oolau bird's Orb and Jemil scenes. Talk and Whisper in a P08 room first ask who speaks when the party has more than one member (the original uses the acting member). The new day's P92 events are built with it (`_disasterday` + 7 → event 1, `_juldate` 20 → event 114, month 9 → event 27). The ending and the epilogue (`main.ts` *ending*, *epilogue*) and Give with the Orb (`main.ts` *offer*, *orb*; `economy.md`, Giving) are built too; the bird room is the world-map spot at (62, 19) in N. Misor (R36 `locptr`, program 8, room 5, always open). Whisper now refreshes the people in the room, so Jemil can be spoken to once the egg cracks. Untested: the elden answers 2–6. The ending's flash, Zakhad appearing and fading, Zidoni appearing and the doom's blasts are built (`effects.md`). The White Scroll's `_npcsta` bits take Jemil, Shannor and Alex (NPCs 56, 60, 61) out of their homes (the KNPC tables and the knock, `party.away`).

**The ending in detail** (P08 0x772–0x89D, 0xBA7; `ovl_1855(n)` prints the room's location text n): the trigger is Yes to leaving *any* P08 room while event 120 is set (L_06fa → L_0772); the transport flag alone skips it. Event 121, texts 3 and 4 (%o = `_HighStat(0x14)`, the best intelligence). Event 123 clear: the member holding the Orb (`_SearchInv` 0x49) is the speaker of text 5, sound 0x1B, text 6 by the player, `ovl_0c41` redraws the party, comtxt 0x50 "Alas, your quest has failed." and `_restarting`. Event 123 set: text 7, sound 0x1C, Zakhad erased (`0x18B4(1)`), text 8 with %o = `_HighStat(0x1A)` (the best magic), event 122, sound 0x0F, the room's people redrawn (Zidoni), texts 9–12, then `OV_0x1c9 = 0x49` and the first exit rewritten to PGM 1 / var 0x10: `ovl_0f20` flashes (its text 3 is only for the White Scroll, 0x4C) and enters the room behind the location's first door, D16 room 12. HighStat's argument is a HERO offset (0x14 `h_intellg`, 0x1A `h_magic`, 0x20 `h_soulrd`).

**The epilogue** (`ovl_041d` 0x8A0 with `OV_0x1d2`, i.e. any P08 room entered with event 122): song 0x0B, menu 9, hour ≥ 20 → date + 1, hour = 20, `_CalUpd`; room text 0; `ovl_09df`: texts 15, 16; `_CntPar() > 2` → 17 and, for party slots 1–5, `ovl_099d(npc)`: once per companion (flags at cs:0xA5C, the player and the King pre-set) the line `table[npc]` (P08 file 0x97C, 33 bytes) with %o = the companion's `h_fullnm`; any companion with `h_helped & 0x80` not in the party → 18 and their lines; 19, 20. Then text 1, `_SetBit(_events, 0xA6)` (event 166), the music ends, and the loop offers only menu 0x17 (`_XQSRGame`).
